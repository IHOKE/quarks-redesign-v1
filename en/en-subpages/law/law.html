Changing the HKID Gender Marker does not reflect changes on legal gender

Updating the gender marker on the HKID card does NOT update one's legal gender. According to IMMD "Guideline on the application for change of sex entry on Hong Kong identity card" The gender marker on the HKID card does not imply / represent that the holder has legally changed their gender, and is not formally recognized for all legal purposes.

Marriage Registration

After the 2013 W v Registrar of Marriages judicial review victory, transgender individuals who have undergone Sex Reassignment Surgery (SRS) can be recognized for marriage registration. For example, a transgender man whose gender marker on the HKID card is updated to Male according to Criterion 1 can marry a woman; however, if they had updated their gender marker with accordance to Criterion 2 or 3, they would still be unable to marry a woman.
